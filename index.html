Welcome to StudyFlow

One friendly, private app that lives entirely in your browser to help you track your time, race the clock, wake up on schedule, and never forget what matters — no accounts, no servers, just you and your day.

✨ How this helps your daily life

  • 📊 Time Tracker — see exactly where your hours go across categories, build daily streaks, and hit goals you set for yourself.
  • ⏳ Timer — run focused countdowns for study, work, workouts, or breaks, one per category.
  • ⏰ Alarm — get woken up or alerted at a precise future time, with a sound you pick.
  • 🔔 Reminder — leave yourself a note on any future day and get notified the moment it arrives.
  • 🌍 World Clock — see the current time in any country you search, side by side.
  • ✅ Planner — a simple checklist for today; anything left unchecked just carries on to tomorrow.
